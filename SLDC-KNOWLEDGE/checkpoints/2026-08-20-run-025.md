## Contradiction / falsification pass

1. **Versioning versus lineage:** DVC/lakeFS version identity is not represented as lineage; OpenLineage lineage is not represented as durable byte-for-byte artifact retention.
2. **Lineage versus correctness:** a complete lineage graph can describe an incorrect computation.
3. **Run completion versus quality:** OpenLineage `COMPLETE` is not treated as validation success.
4. **Version identity versus semantic validity:** an immutable hash/commit identifies what was used, not whether the data was appropriate, unbiased, licensed or leakage-free.
5. **Revert versus side effects:** lakeFS repository-state revert is not generalized to external consumers/copies/notifications/models.
6. **Orchestration versus lineage completeness:** pipeline/DAG success is not evidence that every external dependency was instrumented.
7. **DVC versus lakeFS:** the two tools have materially different workflow/scaling/integration models; no universal replacement/superiority claim is made.
8. **AI authority:** machine-readable version/lineage interfaces support bounded AI-assisted investigation, but no checked source establishes autonomous data-promotion authority.
